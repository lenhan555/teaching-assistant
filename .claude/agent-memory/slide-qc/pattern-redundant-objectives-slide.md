---
name: pattern-redundant-objectives-slide
description: SQL (and likely other subject) decks include a Learning Objectives slide that duplicates the Agenda slide content — creates a redundant opening that slows the narrative arc
metadata:
  type: project
---

In sql-joins-for-beginners (reviewed 2026-05-31), Slide 3 (Learning Objectives) listed 7 objectives that were nearly word-for-word mirrors of the 7 Agenda items on Slide 2. Both slides answered "what will we cover" — one in topic form, one in "you will be able to" form.

**Why this matters:** The opening sequence burned two dark-background slides (Slides 2 and 3 both dark — possible background alternation concern) and delayed the motivating "why" content. Students skip mentally to the content; a second objectives slide adds no value.

**Fix applied:** Replaced Slide 3 with a "Why JOINs Matter" motivator slide that gives students a reason to care before the technical content begins.

**How to apply:** When a deck has both an Agenda slide AND a Learning Objectives slide in the first 4 slides:
- Flag as Major / Structure & Flow issue
- Recommend: either merge objectives into Agenda slide (as sub-items), or replace the Objectives slide with a motivating "Why X Matters" context slide
- Keep the Agenda slide — remove or repurpose the Objectives slide
